FAQ

Straight answers before you start.

Is it free to try?

Yes. The ZeroFriction coach is free to try during this demonstration.

How much effort does it take?

Five quick questions across six steps, plus one optional document example. The example is only needed if you want suggestions shaped around your own paperwork.

Does ZeroFriction make decisions for me?

No. You review and approve every recommendation. It does not send messages, approve financial decisions or replace professional judgement.

What data is saved?

Answers are stored in this browser's local storage. If you attach files, they are processed by Google's Gemini service to write your plan and deleted afterwards. The assessment stores only whether a document was attached and the file count, not the files. The completed assessment and plan are saved in Supabase. Use fictional or redacted documents, not real customer information.
